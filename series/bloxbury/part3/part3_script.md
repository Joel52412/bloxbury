# BLOXBURY Part 3: "Don't Say It" (draft v1, 22 Nov)
Retells the viral **mimic** trend (TikTok #mimic: something copying voices) + the family **secret word** rule, made wrong.
Reframe: Part 2 ended with fake Mum saying "Bug?" Hours earlier it didn't know that word. It learned it from real Mum's whisper.
**It learns every word it hears.** Second reframe: the closet voice we all trusted in Part 2 is the one that gets the word.

## Beat sheet (~70 s)
0. Bridge 0-3 s: open on Part 2's final beat. Black, three soft knocks, light under Milo's bedroom door, two shadow feet.
   Fake Mum (through door): "Bug?" / Milo N: "It called me Bug."  (hook line + picture)
1. 3-11 s  "Last night it didn't know that word. Then it heard Mum say it." Milo clamps a hand over his mouth.
   Open Q: what else has it heard?  Rule stated: it learns every word it hears.
2. 11-21 s Closet crack, Mum's bracelet hand. Whisper: "Don't say anything. Not one word."
   Pattern break: Mum's three rules: brush your teeth / lock your door / never say our secret word out loud.
   Rewatch bait 1: the word LIGHTHOUSE is hidden on Milo's crayon drawing on the wall (props4 drawing, generated image).
3. 21-31 s Door. Fake Mum, too sweet: "If it's really you in there... tell me our word." MUSIC OUT.
   Milo N: "It wasn't checking me. It was learning."  Open Q: how do you talk without words?
4. 31-40 s Milo writes on paper, slides it into the closet crack. Pause. It slides back in Mum's handwriting: "WINDOW. RAE'S. GO."
5. 40-50 s Out the window, across the roof, silent run through night Bloxbury (props5 town).
   Rewatch bait 2: look back at the house: a figure at his bedroom window AND a figure in the closet-side window (4 frames).
6. 50-62 s Rae opens the door, torch + WHO'S LEFT list. Milo can't speak, hand over mouth.
   Rae D: "Milo? You just called me." / "You said the word. Lighthouse."
   Milo N: "I never said it out loud. I only wrote it down once." / "And I gave it to the closet."
   (Comment payoff, optional: "Most of you told me to trust the closet. So did I.")
7. 62-66 s Silence. Rae's phone buzzes. Milo's own voice on speaker: "Rae, it's me. Don't let him in." -> hard cut to black.
   (the one earned startle = a quiet line in his own voice, no sting)
8. 66-72 s Outro to camera (lip-sync): "One of us is on the porch. One of us is on the phone. Which Milo would you let in?"
Loop: ends on three soft knocks on Rae's door -> first frame's three knocks.

## Open decisions
- Secret word: LIGHTHOUSE (easy to read on a drawing, not a common word).
- Keep the "Most of you told me..." comment-payoff line? Only if Part 2 comments mostly picked the closet.
- Mimic voice = Milo's voice + phone FX + slightly too even pacing (no new voice needed).
- Where is real Mum? Deliberately unanswered (Part 4 hook).
