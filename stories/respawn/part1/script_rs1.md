# RESPAWN Part 1 "Checkpoint" (rs1) script v1: ~115 s raw speech, ~1:45 after the 1.1× speed-up
Voices: THEO (narrator, voiceover; mouth moves only on in-scene lines), WREN, BRAMBLE, THE HAND (whisper, red captions).
Deaths are always implied: cut to black on the moment, then the respawn sound. Nothing graphic. Times are raw, before the speed-up.

## Beat sheet
| Time | Beat | Open question |
|---|---|---|
| 0-6 s | Hook: respawn ring, "fourth time I've died today", the same apple drops | Why does he keep dying? |
| 6-18 s | How he got here: a 1-player server at 3 AM | What is Lanternfall? |
| 18-40 s | Loop 1: meets Wren, the stolen Sun Lantern, the last bell | What comes when the bell rings? |
| 40-50 s | First death in the alley (a white glove, 4 frames: rewatch detail) | Who did it? |
| 50-75 s | Loops 2-3 montage: he can't save her, and nobody remembers | Can he change anything? |
| 75-100 s | Loop 4: he tells Wren the truth. Time freezes. The hand. | What is the hand? |
| 100-115 s | Black. Someone says his name. Choice question, loops to line 1 | Who else remembers? |
