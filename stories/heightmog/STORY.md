# HEIGHT MOG (comedy, one-off)
In this town, if someone is taller than you, you have to obey them. Kai, the shortest kid in town, gets mogged by everyone, until he tricks Tower, the tallest kid ever, into kneeling for a dropped Robux. For one second Kai is taller, and he says "Stay down."
