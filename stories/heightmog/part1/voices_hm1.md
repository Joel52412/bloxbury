## New voices (ElevenLabs Voice Design, guidance ~35%)
### KAI (narrator + in-scene lines)
Prompt:
American English, General American accent. A boy around 11 years old with a bright, slightly nasal, mid-high voice. Studio-quality recording. A deadpan, sarcastic kid narrator. Dry, confident and a little fed up. He talks quickly and matter-of-factly, landing every joke with a flat, unimpressed delivery, and can snap into a sudden loud, bossy command.
Preview text:
Okay, so here's the thing about my town. Nobody listens to me. Not my teacher, not my mom, not even the dog. Honestly? I'm used to it. But one day, I'm going to be the one giving the orders. You'll see.
Test line: "Stay. Down." (bossy)

### TOWER (giant kid)
Prompt:
American English, General American accent. A teenage boy around 13 with an unexpectedly deep, low, booming voice. Studio-quality recording. A gentle giant of very few words. Calm, blank and a little sleepy. He speaks very slowly with long pauses, each word heavy and flat.
Preview text:
Hey... little guy. Those look... good. I'm gonna... need those. Thanks. You're... nice. I like you. Okay... bye.
Test line: "...Yes, sir." (defeated)

### PIP (Kai's little sister)
Prompt:
American English, General American accent. A little girl around 6 years old with a tiny, high, sweet voice. Studio-quality recording. A bossy little sister. Smug, demanding and cute. She speaks in short, clipped words with total confidence.
Preview text:
No. That's mine now. Give it. Give it here. Thank you. Now go away, I'm busy. Mom said I'm in charge.
If Voice Design won't make a child voice, ask for "a petite young woman with a very high, childlike voice" instead.
