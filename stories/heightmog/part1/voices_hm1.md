### KAI (narrator + in-scene lines)
Prompt (ElevenLabs blocks child voices, so design an adult and pitch it up ~+3 semitones, formants kept, in our mix):
American English, General American accent. A petite young man in his early 20s with a light, high, boyish, slightly nasal voice. Studio-quality recording. A deadpan, sarcastic narrator. Dry, confident and a little fed up. He talks quickly and matter-of-factly, landing every joke with a flat, unimpressed delivery, and can drop into a silly, forced deep voice.
Preview text:
Okay, so here's the thing about my town. Nobody listens to me. Not my teacher, not my mom, not even the dog. Honestly? I'm used to it. But one day, I'm going to be the one giving the orders. You'll see.
Test line: "Taxes... cancelled." (forced deep voice)

### MAYOR (top guy in the coat)
Prompt (no politics words: ElevenLabs blocks "mayor"/"citizens"/"taxes" in Voice Design):
American English, General American accent. A short man in his 40s with a booming, deep, chesty voice that sounds slightly forced. Studio-quality recording. A pompous cartoon show-off. Arrogant, theatrical and self-important. He speaks slowly and grandly, stretching words for effect, like every sentence is a big announcement.

Test line: "...Please don't tell." (voice cracks small and squeaky; if it can't crack enough, we pitch it up in the mix)

### GUYS (middle + bottom guy, one voice)
Prompt:
American English, General American accent. A man in his 30s with a muffled-sounding, nasal, mid-range voice. Studio-quality recording. A grumpy, uncomfortable sidekick. Whiny, tired and a bit silly. He speaks in short, complaining mutters, like he's squashed in a small space.
